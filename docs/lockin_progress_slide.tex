\begin{columns}[T,totalwidth=\textwidth]
\begin{column}{0.50\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Why park on the flanks instead of re-sweeping}
\scriptsize
The reference ODMR sweep tells us where each dip sits. We park at
$f_-$ and $f_+$ on the steep flanks (one MW frequency on each side of
$f_{0,k}$). When the dip shifts by $\Delta f$, the two parked
intensities respond in \emph{opposite} directions: one flank exits the
dip (intensity rises), the other gets pulled deeper into it (intensity
falls). The signed difference $S_+(t)-S_-(t)$ encodes both the
\emph{sign} and the \emph{size} of $\Delta f$ in a single scalar — no
re-sweeping needed.
\end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.50\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Slope is the conversion factor}
\scriptsize
The rate at which $S_+-S_-$ changes per MHz of dip shift is the slope
difference $m_- - m_+$ (units: intensity\,/\,MHz). It is a property of
the dip's \emph{shape}, captured once during the reference ODMR sweep.
The same intensity drop on a sharp dip vs.\ a broad dip implies very
different $\Delta f$, so without the slope you cannot turn an intensity
error into a frequency error. Slide 2 shows the formal derivation.
\end{block}
\end{column}
\end{columns}

\begin{frame}{Math 2/2: Linearization, $\Delta f$ Formula, and Example}
\vspace{-0.25em}
\begin{columns}[T,totalwidth=\textwidth]
\begin{column}{0.54\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Linearize the spectrum at the parked points}
\small
The dip shape depends only on $(f-f_0)$. A small shift
$f_0 \to f_0 + \Delta f$ changes the intensity at fixed $f$ to first
order by
\[
I(f,\,f_0+\Delta f)
\approx I(f,\,f_0)
- \tfrac{\partial I}{\partial f}\big|_{f_0}\,\Delta f .
\]
At the two parked points this gives
\[
S_-(t) \approx b_- - m_-\,\Delta f, \qquad
S_+(t) \approx b_+ - m_+\,\Delta f,
\]
and subtracting cancels common-mode drift in $b_\pm$:
\[
\underbrace{(S_+ - S_-)_{\rm now}}_{\Delta d_{\rm now}}
\;-\;\underbrace{(b_+ - b_-)}_{\Delta d_{\rm ref}}
=(m_- - m_+)\,\Delta f .
\]
Solve for $\Delta f$:
\[
\boxed{\;\Delta f_k =
\dfrac{\Delta d_{\rm now}-\Delta d_{\rm ref}}{m_- - m_+},
\qquad
f_{{\rm new},k}=f_{0,k}+\Delta f_k\;}
\]
\end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.44\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Symbol legend and sanity checks}
\scriptsize
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
  \item $b_\pm$: normalized PL at $f_\pm$ from the reference ODMR sweep.
  \item $m_\pm = \mathrm{d}I/\mathrm{d}f$ at $f_\pm$. Notebook asserts
        $m_- < 0 < m_+$ (left flank descends, right flank ascends), so
        the denominator $m_- - m_+ < 0$.
  \item $\Delta d_{\rm now} = S_+(t)-S_-(t)$ from the latest batch;
        $\Delta d_{\rm ref} = b_+ - b_-$ from the calibration sweep.
  \item Sign: positive $\Delta f$ from panel (a) gives
        $\Delta d_{\rm now}<\Delta d_{\rm ref}$, i.e.\ negative numerator.
        Negative $\div$ negative $=$ positive $\Delta f$. \checkmark
  \item Symmetric-flank limit ($m_-=-m_+$) recovers the textbook
        $\Delta f = (\Delta d_{\rm now}-\Delta d_{\rm ref})/(2m_-)$.
  \item Field projection along this transition's NV axis:
        $\Delta B_{{\rm proj},k} = \Delta f_k / \|\mathrm{d}f/\mathrm{d}B\|_k$.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
